Privacy & Data Safeguards

Your privacy is paramount. Self-assessments and tools on this kiosk operate without requiring student IDs, logins, or roll numbers. All interaction data remains within your active local session, and an automatic timeout clears the screen after inactivity to protect your confidentiality.

health_and_safety

Mental Well-Being Notice

The self-assessments, guided breathing, and coping resources on this platform are designed for self-reflection and stress management. They do not replace professional mental health evaluation, medical diagnosis, or therapy. If you need urgent assistance, contact Tele-MANAS at 14416 or visit campus health services.
