5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding

When overwhelmed by coursework or anxiety, this technique grounds your senses in the present moment. Tap each step as you complete it:

5

5 things you can SEE

Look around the room: notice the light, a leaf outside, the library table, a clock.

4

4 things you can TOUCH

Feel your shoes on the floor, the texture of your notebook, or the coolness of the kiosk.

3

3 things you can HEAR

Listen for campus sounds: distant wind, someone walking, air circulation.

2

2 things you can SMELL

Notice the air in the room, library paper, rain, or fresh morning grass.

1

1 thing you can TASTE

Take a sip of water, or take note of a calm, refreshing breath.

Take all the time you need.

Campus Self-Help Toolkits

Managing High-Stakes Exam & Lab Pressures

Engineering coursework presents dense deadlines. When work piles up, try the 25/5 Sprint:

  • Break large projects into single micro-tasks: Outline just one problem statement rather than the whole assignment.
  • Protect the first 45 minutes of morning: Avoid checking notification feeds; complete one hard problem first.
  • Mid-Sem Stress Reset: If a test didn't go well, consult the course mentor during faculty office hours. Progress is iterative.

Sleep Strategies for Engineering Students

Late night coding or lab files disrupt sleep quality. Simple adjustments make a noticeable difference:

  • Screen cutoff 30 minutes before bed: Allow your eyes to decompress from monitor glare.
  • Dim ambient hostel room lighting: Warmer light prompts your brain's natural melatonin production.
  • Write down tomorrow's top 3 tasks: Transferring worries from your mind onto paper relieves overnight cognitive churning.

Peaceful Campus Spaces at BVM

Need a silent spot between lectures? These spaces provide restorative quiet:

  • BVM Central Library 2nd Floor: Silent study desks with natural window daylight.
  • Botanical Lawn & Fountain Garden: Shaded benches under palm trees (behind main porch).
  • Auditorium Courtyard: Peaceful breeze corridor in the afternoon.
